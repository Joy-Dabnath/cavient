EHR Capabilities

A Chart That Works as Hard as You Do

Cavient EHR was built with clinician input from day one — every workflow, template, and shortcut is designed to get you out of the chart faster.

AI-Assisted Documentation

Intelligent note generation suggests SOAP, DAP, and BIRP entries based on your session type and specialty. Review, edit, and sign — no more staring at a blank page.

300+ Clinical Templates

Specialty-specific templates for mental health, primary care, home health, and more. Fully customizable with smart fields, conditional logic, and auto-populated patient data.

Validated Clinical Measures

PHQ-9, GAD-7, AUDIT, Columbia Suicide Severity, and 40+ validated assessments auto-scored and graphed over time. Patient can complete assessments online before the visit.

e-Prescribing (EPCS)

Prescribe controlled and non-controlled medications electronically with DEA-compliant two-factor authentication. Drug interaction checks and formulary lookup included.

Lab & Diagnostic Orders

Send lab orders directly to Quest, LabCorp, and hundreds of regional labs. Results flow back into the chart automatically, alerting you to abnormal values in real time.

Interoperability & FHIR

HL7 FHIR R4 compliant with USCDI v2 data sharing. Connect to hospitals, labs, pharmacies, and national health networks including CommonWell and Carequality.
