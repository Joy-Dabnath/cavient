Our Story

Built by Clinicians,
Designed for Care

Cavient was founded in 2018 in Columbia, SC, by a team of healthcare operators who were frustrated by clunky, disconnected software that slowed down clinical care. We built the platform we wished we had.

Our Mission

Healthcare Deserves Better Software

We believe that when clinicians spend less time on administration, they have more time for patients. Cavient's integrated EHR, telehealth, practice management, and billing platform is designed to eliminate the friction that costs practices time and money.

Our CEO, Toyin Bello, founded Cavient after seeing firsthand how disconnected systems were undermining the quality of care. Today, Cavient serves thousands of providers across mental health, primary care, specialty care, and home health.
