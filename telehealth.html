The Telehealth Flow

Simple for Patients.
Powerful for Providers.

From booking to billing, every step of the virtual visit is automated and fully connected to your existing workflow.

1

Patient Books a Video Visit

Patients self-schedule telehealth visits through the patient portal or your website. Insurance eligibility is verified instantly at the time of booking.

2

Automated Reminder with Link

24 hours and 15 minutes before the session, the patient receives a personalized reminder with a one-click join link — no app needed, no account to create.

3

Secure Session in Your Platform

Provider and patient connect directly inside Cavient. Notes draft in the background, vital signs can be recorded, and labs can be ordered — all within the session.

4

Auto-Billing & Documentation

After the session ends, the note is signed, the claim is submitted, and the patient receives their after-visit summary — completely automated.

No-Download Patient Experience

Patients join directly from a browser link. No app store visits, no account creation, no confusion. Works on iOS, Android, and desktop equally well.

Waiting Room Management

Patients wait in a branded virtual lobby. Providers see who is waiting, can send messages, and admit patients when ready — just like a real front desk.

Multi-State Licensure Support

Cavient tracks provider licensure by state and automatically validates that patients are located in a jurisdiction where the provider is licensed at session start.

Telehealth-Specific Billing Codes

Cavient automatically selects the correct CPT/POS codes for telehealth visits (95, 02, 10) based on service type and payer — maximizing reimbursement, minimizing denials.
