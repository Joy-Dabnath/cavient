Core Capabilities

Everything Your Practice Needs to Thrive

From first patient contact to final billing, Cavient streamlines every step of practice operations with intelligent automation.

Intelligent Scheduling

Smart calendar that prevents double-booking, auto-fills cancellations from a waitlist, and sends automated reminders via SMS and email to reduce no-shows by up to 68%.

Digital Patient Intake

Patients complete intake forms digitally before their appointment. Insurance verification, consent forms, and health history collected automatically — zero paper, zero delay.

Multi-Location Management

Manage unlimited clinic locations from a single dashboard. Each site gets its own scheduling, staff, and reporting while sharing a unified patient record system.

Staff & Resource Management

Configure provider schedules, room assignments, and equipment bookings. Role-based permissions ensure every team member has exactly the access they need — nothing more.

Automated Reminders & Follow-ups

Configurable reminder workflows send appointment confirmations, follow-up care instructions, and reactivation campaigns to keep your schedule full and patients engaged.

Operational Analytics

Track utilization rates, no-show patterns, revenue per visit, and provider productivity. Exportable reports for payroll, board meetings, or compliance submissions.

Streamlined Workflow

From Booking to
Billing in Minutes

Cavient's workflow engine connects every step of the patient journey so nothing falls through the cracks.

1

Patient Schedules Online

Patients self-book through your branded portal or you schedule on their behalf. Insurance is verified in real time, and intake forms are sent immediately.

2

Intake Completed Digitally

Demographics, consent forms, and clinical history are collected before the visit and auto-populated into the EHR — no scanning, no transcribing.

3

Provider Sees the Patient

Provider arrives to a fully prepped chart. Clinical notes, treatment plans, and orders are created with AI-assisted templates — cutting documentation time by 50%.

4

Claims Filed Automatically

Visit data flows directly to billing. Claims are scrubbed, submitted, and tracked — reducing denials and accelerating reimbursements to 2–5 business days.
