How It Works

Revenue Cycle on Autopilot

From eligibility check at scheduling to final payment posting — Cavient automates every step of your revenue cycle so your staff can focus on patients, not paperwork.

1

Verify Before the Appointment

Eligibility and benefits are verified automatically at booking. Staff see coverage details, copays, and deductible status before the patient walks in.

2

Scrub & Submit Claims Instantly

Claims are auto-generated from the EHR, scrubbed against 3,000+ payer-specific edits, and submitted electronically in real time — typically within minutes of visit completion.

3

Post Payments Automatically

ERA files are downloaded and matched automatically. Payments, adjustments, and patient balances are posted without manual data entry.

4

Resolve Denials with One Click

Denied claims are flagged, categorized by root cause, and routed to the right workflow. Appeal letters are generated automatically using denial-specific templates.
